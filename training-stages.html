Sample itinerary

What a 7-night stage looks like

Every stage is tailored in length and intensity — this is the full Élite program. Shorter plans keep the same structure with fewer days.

01
Arrival

Welcome to Valencia

Airport pickup, check-in, welcome lunch and a first introductory session at L'Alqueria with Valencia Basket Academy coaches.

02–03
Immersion

Intensive training & physical preparation

Two daily sessions: technical training, strength and conditioning, video-room tactical review, and friendly matches against local Valencian teams each afternoon.

04
Culture

Architecture, cuisine, sea and culture

City of Arts and Sciences, Oceanogràfic, traditional Valencian paella by the Turia riverbed, and free time at Malvarrosa beach.

05
Elite day

Training with the Academy & a pro game at Roig Arena

1x1 to 5x5 session with certified coaches, lunch at L'Alqueria, and an official ACB League or EuroLeague game live at Roig Arena.

06–07
Closing

Analysis and return

Final session, shared reflection, and private transfer to the airport — with a performance report, a digital photo/video album and a personalized improvement plan for every player.
